In-person registration

Registration requirements

Register at the Barangay Office

Resident accounts are created in person for verification. Visit Barangay Luna, Surigao City, Surigao del Norte, Philippines during office hours with the documents below. Bring originals for validation and photocopies where applicable.

  • Valid Government-Issued ID

    Required

    Original and photocopy of any primary ID (e.g., National ID, Passport, Driver's License, UMID, Voter's ID, SSS/GSIS, PRC).

  • Proof of Residence

    Required

    At least one recent document showing your address within Barangay Luna.

    • Electricity bill (MERALCO or local cooperative)
    • Water bill
    • Internet / cable subscription bill
    • Lease contract or barangay certification of residency

  • Barangay Certificate

    Required

    Barangay Certificate or Barangay Clearance issued by Barangay Luna, if required by the front desk.

  • Birth Certificate

    Required

    PSA-issued birth certificate (original or certified true copy) for identity verification.

  • Other Supporting Documents

    If applicable

    Additional papers that may be requested depending on your application type or civil status.

    • Marriage certificate (if applicable)
    • Authorization letter and valid ID of representative (if applying on behalf of another person)
    • Recent 1x1 or 2x2 ID photo
    • Any other document specified by the Barangay Secretary
