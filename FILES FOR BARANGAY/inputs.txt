Use these inputs/placeholders inside your Word `.docx` template:

{{resident_name}}

Resident name in ALL CAPS.


{{resident_name_proper}}

Resident name in normal format.


{{resident_address}}

Resident address.


{{age}}

Resident age.


{{gender}}

Resident gender.


{{civil_status}}

Resident civil status.


{{purpose}}

Purpose entered when issuing the document.


{{date_issued}}

Full date, example: `June 25, 2026`.


{{date_day}}

Day only, example: `25`.


{{date_day_ordinal}}

Day with suffix, example: `25th`.


{{date_month}}

Month name, example: `June`.


{{date_month_upper}}

Month in ALL CAPS, example: `JUNE`.


{{date_year}}

Year only, example: `2026`.


{{barangay_name}}

Outputs `Barangay Luna`.


{{barangay_captain}}

Barangay captain text.


{{document_number}}

Document control number.

{{pronoun_subject}}

Outputs `He` or `She`.


{{pronoun_subject_lower}}

Outputs `he` or `she`.


{{pronoun_object}}

Outputs `him` or `her`.


{{pronoun_possessive}}

Outputs `his` or `her`.

Example:
{{pronoun_subject}} is known to me and {{pronoun_possessive}} purpose is {{purpose}}.

Result:
He is known to me and his purpose is SCHOOL REQUIREMENT.
